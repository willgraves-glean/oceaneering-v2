1. What is Glean?

Glean is a Work AI platform built to connect large language models and agents to your company’s full enterprise context – data, people, and processes – with governance and security built in from day one.

  • System of context: Glean indexes and unifies content, metadata, permissions, and activity signals across 100+ SaaS and data systems into a single, AI-ready index.
  • Enterprise & personal graphs: rich knowledge graphs capture how people, projects, assets, and systems relate – plus personal graphs that learn how each user works while respecting existing access controls.
  • Horizontal assistant & agents: a single assistant and agent platform that works across Microsoft 365, engineering tools, ERP/CRM, ticketing, and more.
  • Open by design: bring your own cloud, models, and third‑party agents via open standards (MCP, LangChain Agent Protocol, and others) – without re‑indexing data.
  • Deployment flexibility: SaaS single‑tenant or customer‑managed (Azure, AWS, or GCP), with zero‑data‑retention options and strict control over what leaves your tenant.
  • Glean Protect: end‑to‑end security and governance for AI – from permissions‑aware indexing and sensitive content policies to agent guardrails and protection against prompt injection.
  • For builders & business users: natural‑language agent builder and open APIs so teams can create governed agents without re‑implementing plumbing around data, context, and security.

2. Tailored value for Oceaneering

Based on your public disclosures and our recent discovery with the Chief Data Officer, we see four focus areas where Glean can be an immediate force‑multiplier for Oceaneering’s AI strategy.

2.1. Make AI governance an accelerant, not a brake

  • Unify permissions once: mirror Oceaneering’s existing identity and application‑level permissions (including RBAC and row‑level controls where available) into a single policy layer that search, chat, and agents all respect by default.
  • Enterprise & AI governance together: combine data governance (who can see what), AI governance (which models, which use‑cases), and agent governance (who can build and run what) in one place – rather than scattering controls across tools.
  • Measured rollout: start with constrained domains (e.g., engineering knowledge, internal policies, IMDS asset integrity content) while Glean Protect continuously monitors for sensitive content and policy violations.

2.2. Connect Oracle Fusion & legacy ERP into AI workflows

  • Structured + unstructured in one place: let leaders ask questions that span ERP (budget to actuals, unpaid invoices, project margin) and unstructured context (contracts, SoWs, engineering reports, maintenance logs) in a single, governed experience.
  • ERP as a first‑class signal: use structured data to flag risk – e.g., projects where spend lags plan and a related vendor invoice is stuck – and route these as prioritized work items via agents.
  • Respect existing investments: Glean doesn’t replace your data platform or Copilot investments; it provides a horizontal context and governance layer they can plug into.

2.3. Safely move from specialist agents to broader adoption

  • From core AI team to citizen builders: give your existing agent builders a governed platform (model hub, agent builder, orchestration, observability) and then selectively open up “low‑risk lanes” for business users to assemble agents from approved building blocks.
  • Personal graphs for key roles: CDO, ADTech leaders, IMDS teams, and regional ops leaders each get assistants and agents tuned to their projects, collaborators, and data – without breaking least‑privilege.
  • Guardrails by design: human‑in‑the‑loop approvals, scoped actions, and sensitive‑content controls so agents can take work off the team’s plate without increasing risk surface.

2.4. Support Oceaneering’s shift toward industrial tech & defense

  • Operational excellence at scale: use Glean to codify best‑practice playbooks across subsea robotics, defense programs, and terrestrial automation – so lessons from one business line are discoverable and reusable across the portfolio.
  • Safety‑first automation: ground AI and agents in authoritative procedures, asset integrity rules, and safety policies so “AI‑assisted” workflows always respect Oceaneering’s safety posture.
  • Faster ramp on new programs: help new teams traverse decades of historical project data, reports, and procedures quickly, shortening time to productivity on new defense and autonomy programs.

4. Why Glean – Governance, Enterprise Context, and Openness

Three pillars matter most for Oceaneering’s next phase: governance you can trust, complete enterprise context, and an open platform that plays well with your existing stack (Microsoft, Oracle, Snowflake/Databricks, custom agents, and beyond).

4.1. Governance – security, permissions, and AI safety by design

  • Single source of truth for permissions: Glean enforces the same permissions your source systems define – from identity provider and group membership down to record‑ and object‑level controls – and keeps them in near real time, so there is no parallel “policy universe” to manage.
  • Single‑tenant & cloud‑prem options: deploy Glean as a dedicated single‑tenant SaaS instance or run it in your own cloud (Azure, AWS, or GCP) to align with Oceaneering’s data residency and regulatory requirements.
  • Zero‑retention model options: use Glean’s universal model key with zero‑data‑retention contracts, or bring your own keys and model providers so you control what leaves your tenant and under which terms.
  • Glean Protect: sensitive‑content policies, classifiers that continuously hide or flag risky material, protection against prompt‑injection and jailbreak attempts, and agent‑level guardrails (who can build, share, or auto‑run agents, and when human approval is required).

4.2. Enterprise context – one unified index & graph over Oceaneering’s work

  • Unified index across 100+ systems: Glean creates a horizontal index over documents, messages, tickets, records, code, and more – including rich metadata such as ownership, usage, and authority – so AI can work over how your business actually operates, not just isolated files.
  • Enterprise graph & personal graphs: a deep knowledge graph maps relationships between people, teams, customers, projects, assets, and systems; per‑user personal graphs capture work patterns and preferences while still honoring least‑privilege access.
  • Structured + unstructured: Glean bridges structured data (e.g., ERP, CRM, data warehouses) with unstructured context (reports, drawings, procedures, email, chat) so agents can reason over both the numbers and the “why” behind them.
  • Evaluation‑driven retrieval: our system of context continuously evaluates and refines which sources to retrieve from and how to ground answers, reducing hallucinations and surfacing citations so teams can verify results.

4.3. Openness – fit to Oceaneering’s hybrid stack and agent ecosystem

  • Open model hub: access 15+ leading models via a universal key or your own keys, and choose per‑use‑case or per‑step which model to invoke – balancing accuracy, cost, and risk without vendor lock‑in.
  • Open agent platform: Glean supports open standards like Anthropic’s Model Context Protocol (MCP) and LangChain’s Agent Protocol so agents built in other frameworks can call into Glean for enterprise context – and Glean agents can call out to external tools and agents.
  • Index once, use everywhere: your data is indexed and secured one time in Glean’s system of context, then reused across Glean Assistant, departmental agents, custom applications, MCP‑enabled IDEs, and third‑party agent frameworks.
  • Extensible via APIs: open REST and SDK interfaces so your teams can embed Glean’s search, assistant, and agents into existing portals, internal apps, or partner experiences without rebuilding the foundation.
